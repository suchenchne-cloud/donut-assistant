## Why Donut Assistant?

Whether you are flipping goods, planning a farm, or trying to understand market movement, Donut Assistant gives you a single place to:

- track auction house and order prices,
- compare profit opportunities,
- review historical market observations,
- estimate potential gains from AH-to-Orders trades,
- and plan smarter long-term farm decisions.

## Features

- Auction House and Orders price tracking
- Farm profit calculator
- Historical market observations
- Experimental AH-to-Orders profit estimates
- Minecraft Fabric mod integration (beta testing)

## Coming soon

- More reliable market scanning

## Project status

Donut Assistant is currently in beta. Market information is based on recorded observations and is not guaranteed to reflect live prices. Profit estimates are informational only and do not guarantee successful trades.

This project is independent and not officially affiliated with Donut SMP.
